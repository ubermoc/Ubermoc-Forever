UbermocForever 1.3.19
- Added: a "Cinematic: On/Off" button in the options window header to turn the cinematic camera on or off.

UbermocForever 1.3.18
- Added: cinematic camera while talking to a quest giver or gossip NPC. The camera moves in and turns to the NPC, black bars slide in, and the rest of the interface fades. The game's own quest and gossip windows are not changed. Everything is put back when the talk ends, in combat, and after a reload. /ubf cinema off turns it off, /ubf cinema on turns it back on.

UbermocForever 1.3.17
- Changed: when the combination you press is already used for something else, the game's own question window asks "Replace it with this spell?" (A replaces, B keeps what is there) instead of asking you to press it again.

UbermocForever 1.3.16
- Changed: when the combination you press is already used for something else (Jump on every layer, for example), pressing the same combination again replaces it with the spell.

UbermocForever 1.3.15
- Added: while a spell is being bound, chat shows every key or button the game sends, so a combination that does not arrive can be seen.

UbermocForever 1.3.14
- Fixed: binding a spell by pressing buttons also takes keyboard keys, so a back button that Steam Input turns into a key (L4 as SPACE, say) works together with a held paddle that stands in for Ctrl or Shift. Escape cancels too.

UbermocForever 1.3.13
- Changed: "Bind to action bar" now works by pressing buttons. After choosing it, hold your paddles and press the button you want (B alone cancels). A combination that already triggers an action slot gets the spell in that slot. A combination with no bind (a back button pair, say) gets the spell in the first empty slot on a visible bar (bars 4 and 5 first) and is bound to that slot. A combination used for something else is left alone and says what it is used for.

UbermocForever 1.3.12
- Fixed: placing a spell now finds every action bar that is showing, also bars with the back buttons bound through Steam Input and custom bars.

UbermocForever 1.3.10
- Fixed: the highlight is no longer anchored to action bar buttons (it is placed by screen position), so the action bars have nothing of ours attached to them when the game moves them.

UbermocForever 1.3.9
- Fixed: placing a spell on an action bar finds the slot of any bar button (also from its name), says in chat what happened, and reports a failure instead of silently doing nothing.

UbermocForever 1.3.8
- Fixed: no tooltip is raised on the action bars any more. Running the bars' own tooltip scripts from the addon left values tainted, which showed as "secret value" cooldown errors and a blocked MainActionBar action after binding a spell.

UbermocForever 1.3.7
- Changed: "Bind to action bar" no longer picks the spell up. The highlight goes over the action bars and picking a slot places the spell in one step, so it never sits on the cursor.

UbermocForever 1.3.6
- Added: the Y menu also offers armor kits for the armor pieces they fit, and scopes for your ranged weapon. Where an item goes is now read from the game's own use text, for the bag menu and for worn items in the character screen.
- Added: /ubf scan (an author tool that saves the use text of items applied to other items).
- Added: the right stick scrolls the list or text under the highlight (a quest's text, a long list), faster the further you push. It only takes the sticks from the game while the highlight is in something that scrolls, and never in combat. /ubf stick flip reverses the direction.
- Fixed: scrolling with the triggers reaches both ends of a list and no longer gets pulled back by the highlight.
- Fixed: moving the highlight through a long list scrolls the list only as far as needed (it no longer jumps to the top or bottom), keeps going when you press up or down at the edge of the view, and never snaps to the window's exit button when a row scrolls away.
- Added: in the mailbox, X on a mail with an item or money takes everything in it (not cash-on-delivery mail, which says so). The legend shows "Take all" there.
- Fixed: the click handlers for drop-downs and buttons that act on mouse down could not see the highlighted button, so they never ran.
- Changed: the highlight is now a glow over the button (the game's own button highlight) instead of a gold outline.

UbermocForever 1.3.5
- Added: quest and gossip windows start the highlight on the first option, or on the Accept, Continue or Complete button. A accepts a quest and B declines it.
- Added: in a trainer, A selects a skill and X trains it.

UbermocForever 1.3.4
- Fixed: the Y menu on a bag item at a vendor now sells the item. Before, Sell used the item, which equipped it.

UbermocForever 1.3.3
- Added: moving around the game's own windows with the controller (bags, character, spellbook, professions, map, mail, game menu, question windows). The D-pad moves a highlight, A and X click, B closes the window the highlight is in and jumps to the next open one, the bumpers change tabs and pages, the triggers step the side tabs down the right of a window (or scroll a list), and the highlight follows new windows, drop-down menus and the open letter in the mail.
- Added: a legend under the window you are in that lists what each button does, wrapping to fit the window. The item's tooltip shows beside the highlight, on whichever side has room.
- Added: Y menu on a bag item (Use or Sell, Use on your main hand or off hand for poisons, stones and oils, Disenchant, Prospect, Mill, Pick up, Delete, Sort bags), on a worn item (Use, the items from your bags that can be used on it, Pick up), and on a spell in the spellbook (Bind to action bar, then pick the slot).
- Added: windows stay open in combat and give the buttons back to your own binds; they will not open in combat.
- Added: /ubf item (what the game says about the highlighted item), /ubf tabs (which tab groups were found), /ubf nav off to turn window navigation off.
- Fixed: X no longer right-clicks buttons in question windows, which made the game log errors.

UbermocForever 1.0.6
- Added: Start steps like Esc on every layer: it closes what is open, clears the target and stops a cast, then opens the game menu, and closes the menu if it is up.
- Added: the legend shows LB / RB for the previous / next hold on the key binds page, and the bumpers wrap from the last hold to the first and back.
- Changed: the window is wider so the legend fits; Choosing Exit in the picker binds the Start button.
- Fixed: Start did nothing on the plain layer when it was the game's own Escape.
