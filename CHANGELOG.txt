UbermocForever 1.3.6
- Added: the Y menu also offers armor kits for the armor pieces they fit, and scopes for your ranged weapon. Where an item goes is now read from the game's own use text, for the bag menu and for worn items in the character screen.
- Added: /ubf scan (an author tool that saves the use text of items applied to other items).

UbermocForever 1.3.5
- Added: quest and gossip windows start the highlight on the first option, or on the Accept, Continue or Complete button. A accepts a quest and B declines it.
- Added: in a trainer, A selects a skill and X trains it.

UbermocForever 1.3.4
- Fixed: the Y menu on a bag item at a vendor now sells the item. Before, Sell used the item, which equipped it.

UbermocForever 1.3.3
- Added: moving around the game's own windows with the controller (bags, character, spellbook, professions, map, mail, game menu, question windows). The D-pad moves a highlight, A and X click, B closes the window the highlight is in and jumps to the next open one, the bumpers change tabs and pages, the triggers step the side tabs down the right of a window (or scroll a list), and the highlight follows new windows, drop-down menus and the open letter in the mail.
- Added: a legend under the window you are in that lists what each button does, wrapping to fit the window. The item's tooltip shows beside the highlight, on whichever side has room.
- Added: Y menu on a bag item (Use or Sell, Use on your main hand or off hand for poisons, stones and oils, Disenchant, Prospect, Mill, Pick up, Delete, Sort bags), on a worn item (Use, the items from your bags that can be used on it, Pick up), and on a spell in the spellbook (Bind to action bar, then pick the slot).
- Added: windows stay open in combat and give the buttons back to your own binds; they will not open in combat.
- Added: /ubf item (what the game says about the highlighted item), /ubf tabs (which tab groups were found), /ubf nav off to turn window navigation off.
- Fixed: X no longer right-clicks buttons in question windows, which made the game log errors.

UbermocForever 1.0.6
- Added: Start steps like Esc on every layer: it closes what is open, clears the target and stops a cast, then opens the game menu, and closes the menu if it is up.
- Added: the legend shows LB / RB for the previous / next hold on the key binds page, and the bumpers wrap from the last hold to the first and back.
- Changed: the window is wider so the legend fits; Choosing Exit in the picker binds the Start button.
- Fixed: Start did nothing on the plain layer when it was the game's own Escape.
