Neither ThePath (extracted in `.../WoW Research and Tools/ThePath-1.6.2`) nor ForeverPlusPlus routes along roads or around obstacles inside a zone; ThePath learns zone-border crossings from players and has a hand-made table of 8 Horde/neutral docks; FarstriderLib (GPL-3.0, `FarstriderLibData-v1794960000` next to it, downloaded and read) is a Dijkstra over a travel graph (flight paths, boats, zeppelins, portals, trams; walking is a straight line with a detour factor) and its Forever ("Camelot") data has only 16 connections: the Deeprun Tram, Menethil-Theramore (as Baradin Bay - Theramore - Menethil), Booty Bay-Ratchet and 8 mage portals, no zeppelins. The Forever guides (Method.gg 2026-10-14, games.gg 2026-11-16) list 13 routes: Alliance Stormwind Harbour-Auberdine, the Auberdine-Menethil-Southshore loop, Menethil-Theramore, Auberdine-Rut'theran, Feathermoon-Forgotten Coast, the Deeprun Tram, the Dalaran skyship to Zephras Isle; Horde Orgrimmar-Undercity, Undercity-Grom'gol, Orgrimmar-Grom'gol and the Skywatcher Plateau skyship; both: Ratchet-Booty Bay and Steamwheedle Port-Powderfuse Port. No dock coordinates anywhere for most of them. So `Ubermoc-Forever/NavScans.lua` reads them from the game: `/ubf docks` (C_AreaPoiInfo over every map, DB.navData.docks.rows = transport-like, .all = every point, .atlases = icon census), `/ubf taxi` (C_TaxiMap.GetTaxiNodesForMap, works anywhere), `/ubf maps`, `/ubf entrances`; each row has the continent id and world position from C_Map.GetWorldPosFromMapPos. Next: the user runs one scan at a time and /reloads; read `navData` in the saved settings to build the transport table (both factions) and the zone geometry for routing.
