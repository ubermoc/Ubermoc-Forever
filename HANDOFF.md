## What was learned this session (so it is not looked up again)
- Details with sources are in `docs/RESEARCH-QUEST-ARROW.md` ("Navigation research"). Short form: the game gives addons NO roads, terrain, water, collision, dock or entrance data (the scans found 16 map points of interest, no docks; the Encounter Journal is off in Forever, so entrances are empty). Neither ThePath nor ForeverPlusPlus routes along roads or around obstacles inside a zone; ThePath learns zone-border crossings from players, FarstriderLib routes over flights, boats, zeppelins and portals (walking is a straight line) and its Forever data has only 16 connections. So this addon learns from play.
- Coordinates: `C_Map.GetWorldPosFromMapPos` gives continent and world position in yards, x to the NORTH and y to the WEST (regression on the maps scan). Bearing counter-clockwise from north is `atan2(dy, dx)`. `GetPlayerFacing` is counter-clockwise from north too, so the arrow turn is bearing minus facing; `SetRotation` turns counter-clockwise.
- Forever has 60 maps (uiMapIDs 947, 1411-1464, 2482, 2521, 2524, 2548, 2652, 2665), continents 1414 Kalimdor and 1415 Eastern Kingdoms, world continent ids 0 (EK) and 1 (Kalimdor). 76 flight masters, faction values 0 neutral, 1 Horde, 2 Alliance.
- Forever's routes by the guides (Method.gg 2026-10-14, games.gg 2026-11-16): Alliance Stormwind Harbour-Auberdine, the Auberdine-Menethil-Southshore loop (one way), Menethil-Theramore, Auberdine-Rut'theran, Feathermoon Stronghold-Forgotten Coast, the Deeprun Tram, Dalaran skyship to Zephras Isle; Horde Orgrimmar-Undercity, Undercity-Grom'gol, Orgrimmar-Grom'gol zeppelins and the Skywatcher Plateau skyship; both: Ratchet-Booty Bay and Steamwheedle Port-Powderfuse Port. No dock coordinates exist anywhere (guides, wiki, FarstriderLib for most); hence ride learning. The names are used only for narration (`Q.RIDEHINT`).
- Licences: this addon is MIT. ThePath (GPL-3.0) and FarstriderLib (GPL-3.0) were read for ideas and nothing was copied from them. ForeverPlusPlus is MIT: its world-map pin approach (a map data provider with plain frames) was followed. Their files are in the research folder (`ThePath-1.6.2`, `FarstriderLibData-v1794960000`, `ForeverPlusPlus`).
- The saved-settings file is written only at `/reload` or logout. Read it with `tools/read_saved.py`.
