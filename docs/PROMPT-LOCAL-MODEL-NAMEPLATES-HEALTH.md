WHAT WENT WRONG IN PHASE 1, AND THE TASK
The user tried phase 1 in the game: "it really just took off the bars and just has the name, but the name is supposed to be the health bar: as they lose health it's supposed to change colors and have a percentage to the right of it with their current health". Phase 1 left that out on purpose (health is a secret value in Forever) and it is now the task. The look, as in Ubermoc UI's defaults (Main.lua 396-404: nameGradient on, missColor WHITE, showPct on, pctColor REACTION, pctPos RIGHT): on a name-only plate, the name IS the health bar. The part of the name that stands for the health left keeps its colour (class colour for players, reaction colour otherwise), the part that stands for the missing health is white, and to the right of the name the health percentage is written, in the reaction colour. Out of scope: level square, threat, auras, icons, cast timers, sizes, a health-tinted percent (see rule 4 below), settings for the missing-health colour (it is white).

THE TECHNIQUE (the game fills a bar from a secret; we never see a number)
1. Our own name text "ours" (a FontString of ours, its width is the width of its text): ours:SetText(UnitName(unit)). SetText accepts a secret (DOCS/SimpleFontStringAPIDocumentation.lua 664-667, AllowedWhenTainted).
2. A hidden StatusBar "sb" of ours, stretched over ours (TOPLEFT and BOTTOMRIGHT anchored to ours), its fill texture invisible (sb:SetStatusBarTexture("Interface\\Buttons\\WHITE8x8"), sb:GetStatusBarTexture():SetVertexColor(1, 1, 1, 0)). sb:SetMinMaxValues(0, 100) and sb:SetValue(UnitHealthPercent(unit, false, CurveConstants.ScaleTo100)). SetValue and SetMinMaxValues accept a secret (DOCS/SimpleStatusBarAPIDocumentation.lua 355-358 and 229-232, AllowedWhenTainted). UnitHealthPercent returns a secret (DOCS/UnitDocumentation.lua 1506-1523). CurveConstants.ScaleTo100 is the game's own curve (BLIZZ/Blizzard_SharedXMLBase/CurveConstants.lua 9-11).
3. A clip frame "clip" of ours with clip:SetClipsChildren(true) (DOCS/SimpleFrameAPIDocumentation.lua 1218, a plain bool): its TOPLEFT is anchored to sb:GetStatusBarTexture() TOPRIGHT and its BOTTOMRIGHT to sb BOTTOMRIGHT, so it covers exactly the part of the name that stands for MISSING health. Inside it a second text "copy" (a FontString of the clip frame) with the same text and the same font as ours, anchored CENTER to ours (as the game's own name does, see step 1 of STEPS), colour white. White shows over the missing part, the colour of ours shows over the rest.
4. A percent text "pct" of ours anchored LEFT to the RIGHT of ours (+4): pct:SetFormattedText("%.0f%%", UnitHealthPercent(unit, false, CurveConstants.ScaleTo100)). SetFormattedText accepts a secret (DOCS/SimpleFontStringAPIDocumentation.lua 539-542, AllowedWhenTainted). Its colour is the reaction colour, plain numbers only: SetTextColor does NOT accept a secret for addon code (DOCS/SimpleFontAPIDocumentation.lua 298-300, AllowedWhenUntainted), so the percent is never tinted by health.
5. Health changes: our own event frame per plate (not a game frame) with RegisterUnitEvent("UNIT_HEALTH", unit) and RegisterUnitEvent("UNIT_MAXHEALTH", unit) (verify both events in DOCS/UnitDocumentation.lua; UNIT_HEALTH is at 4317-4327); the handler only redoes sb:SetValue and the pct text. No global UNIT_HEALTH registration, no OnUpdate.

RULES FOR SECRET VALUES (new, strict; the values from UnitName, UnitClass and UnitHealthPercent can be secret)
1. A unit-data value may only be: passed straight to SetText, SetFormattedText or SetValue; tested with issecretvalue(v); asked type(v). NEVER ==, ~=, <, .., #, if v then, math, string functions, tostring, or used as a table key. To ask "is there a name": type(nm) == "string".
2. Never call GetText, GetStringWidth, GetWidth, GetHeight, GetLeft, GetRight, GetTop, GetBottom or GetCenter on ours, copy, pct, sb, clip or the bar texture: the answer depends on secrets and is a secret.
3. UnitHealth and UnitHealthMax are never used (always secret). Only UnitHealthPercent.
4. Colours are plain numbers: class colour only when UnitIsPlayer(unit) is true AND the class token from UnitClass is a string and not secret (RAID_CLASS_COLORS[token]); otherwise the reaction colour from UnitReaction("player", unit) (plain; <= 2: .85 .15 .15, 4: 1 .85 .1, >= 5: .15 .8 .15, anything else 1 .5 .1, as Main.lua 4790-4802); nothing known: white. A unit that is tapped by others (UnitIsTapDenied true): grey .55 .55 .55.
5. The game's own name must stay visible whenever anything of ours fails: hide the game's name (alpha 0, with the phase 1 helpers N.hide / N.restore so its own alpha comes back) ONLY after ours, sb, clip, copy and pct are made and ours has its text set without an error. Any pcall failure, a missing UnitHealthPercent, a missing CurveConstants.ScaleTo100: clear ours and leave the game's name as it was.

HARD RULES (phase 1 rules, still true for the game's pieces)
- Never call a method of a game piece (SetShowOnlyName, UpdateShownState, UpdateAnchors, SetUnit, UpdateName, Show, Hide, SetShown ...). Never write a field on a game frame or table. Our state lives in our own weak-keyed table keyed by the plate's unit frame: objs[uf] = { ... }.
- No hooksecurefunc. No OnUpdate. No SetPoint, ClearAllPoints, SetFont, SetText on the game's pieces (ours and the game's name are different objects; we may only anchor OUR objects TO the game's name, never the other way round).
- New and allowed: we create OUR OWN frames: CreateFrame("Frame", nil, uf) as a child of the plate's unit frame (so the plate's fading and visibility carry over), and our FontStrings and StatusBar are made from OUR frames, never with uf:CreateFontString. Show and Hide on OUR frames are fine. Our frames never take the mouse.
- Everything that touches a game frame or a secret inside pcall. Every game function or global that might not exist guarded. Lua 5.1 only: no goto, //, bit operators, <const>.
